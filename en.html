The artist behind the name

Jesús Güimil Beiras

Born in Vigo, Spain, with Argentine roots, MK MAIK brings his personal experiences to his creative work. His story speaks of identity, bullying, resilience and the power of music to open a new chapter.

His professional journey includes music releases, live performances and media appearances. He became a professional member of the Latin Recording Academy in 2026. Membership does not imply a Latin GRAMMY nomination or award.
