Chapter 0

The origin

Jesús Güimil Beiras, known as MK MAIK, was born in Vigo and has Argentine roots. His life has been shaped by a search for identity, bullying, resilience and an inner strength that eventually found its voice through music.

His message isn't a performance: it comes from lived experience. Out of silence came his voice, and from that voice, his legacy.

Fans · Community · Purpose

Thank you for giving meaning to my frequency

Every message, every hug and every person who comes up to me after a show reminds me why I keep going. My music doesn't end with the last note: it begins when someone feels less alone.

To the fan who thanked me because my story gave them strength to keep shining after being bullied: thank you. Your courage is part of this story, too.

“If my story helps even one person find the strength to get back up, then all that pain had a purpose.”
